01 / Non-Destructive Testing

Inspection without compromising the asset.

LUKEV provides professional NDT and inspection services for welds, pipelines, pressure vessels, storage tanks, structures and other critical components, using proven techniques and procedures in line with applicable standards and client requirements.

  • Visual Testing (VT)
  • Ultrasonic Testing (UT)
  • Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)
  • Liquid Penetrant Testing (PT)
  • Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT)
  • Thickness Measurement & Corrosion Monitoring
  • Weld Inspection & Evaluation
  • In-Service Inspection
  • Inspection Reporting & Documentation
